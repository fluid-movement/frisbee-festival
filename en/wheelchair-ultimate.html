Inclusion and Action

Wheelchair Ultimate Frisbee

Inclusion in action: this sport doesn't divide – it connects. Wheelchair Ultimate shows how creative, inclusive and fair sport can be when everyone plays together. It is a gripping experience that brings people together and demonstrates that sporting success depends above all on awareness, skill and teamwork.

What is Wheelchair Ultimate Frisbee?

Wheelchair Ultimate is an adapted version of classic Ultimate Frisbee. The core values remain identical: non-contact play, self-officiating and the "Spirit of the Game" come first. Through targeted adaptations for wheelchair users, a deeply inclusive sport is created that promotes agility, strategic thinking and the active participation of people with a wide range of mobility levels.

What Makes It So Special?

It's more than just a game – it's pure energy. The moment you roll across the field, catch the disc and combine with your team, you feel the joy that defines this sport. Wheelchair Ultimate is fast, fair and gripping. Since success depends less on purely physical attributes and more on tactical understanding and cooperation, the sport is accessible and exciting for everyone.

Experience a Whole New Perspective

At the Frisbee Festival Munich we make this sport accessible to everyone. After a show game featuring both experienced players and beginners, our workshop invites everyone — with or without a disability — to get into a sports wheelchair themselves. Discover the challenge of this way of moving, gain new perspectives and enjoy a great game on equal terms. Take a seat, grab the disc and become part of our community!

It's more than just a game – it's an experience that brings people together. The moment you roll across the field, catch the disc and work together with your teammates, you feel the energy and joy that make this sport special. It's fast, fair and exciting. Success depends on awareness, skill and cooperation – not solely on physical ability – which makes it thrilling and accessible to all. Why everyone should try it – with or without a disability: Wheelchair Ultimate offers a new perspective on athletic movement and teamwork. Players without a disability quickly discover the challenge and fun of manoeuvring a wheelchair, and gain a new appreciation for the athleticism of wheelchair users. At the same time, players with a disability enjoy a setting in which they can participate as equals and fully develop their abilities.
