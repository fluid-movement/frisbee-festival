Participate

Watching is great, throwing yourself is better. We give you the chance to experience the fascination of frisbee first-hand. In our workshops, experienced players show you the tricks of the various disciplines – no matter how much experience you bring. Pick your slot below and become part of the festival!

Samstag

Disc Golf

Putting Clinic

09:00 - 10:00 Disc Golf Course

Learn the fundamentals of disc golf putting. Our experienced players show you technique and positioning for precise putts from short and medium distances.

Wheelchair Ultimate

Workshop for Everyone

11:00 - 15:00 Field A

Learn the basics of Wheelchair Ultimate, including throwing techniques, movement strategies and teamwork. Everyone is welcome, whether with or without a disability.

Freestyle

Freestyle Workshop

13:00 - 14:00 Freestyle Field

We'll teach you the basics of Freestyle Frisbee. Everyone is welcome, whether you already play a frisbee sport or have never held a disc before.

Double Disc Court

Open Play

15:30 - 16:00 Field B

Open play for everyone. You can also learn the game here if you're new. There are no fixed teams – just start playing.

Wheelchair Ultimate

Open Play for Everyone

15:30 - 17:00 Field A

After the workshop, all participants can apply what they've learned in an open game.

Disc Golf

Distance Driving Workshop

16:00 - 17:00 Disc Golf Course

Techniques for maximising driving distance. Our pros show you how to get more distance out of your throw with the right technique.

Sonntag

Freestyle

Junior Challenge powered by XDISC

13:00 - 14:00 Freestyle Field

Discover the World of Flying Discs
