Now it gets creative

Freestyle Frisbee

Freestyle Frisbee is the spectacular fusion of acrobatics, dance and precision. To pounding beats, athletes perform choreographed shows that seem to defy gravity. A thrilling experience that perfectly unites sport and art.

Creativity & Teamwork

In freestyle there are no limits. Teams merge into a single unit where each trick builds on the next – as precise as modern ballet. This freedom makes freestyle the most individual and creative discipline in frisbee.

Art in Motion

At its core, freestyle is about manipulating the spinning disc through technically demanding tricks. The basis of almost every manoeuvre is the nail delay: the disc spins on the fingernail. Players combine controlled disc manipulations with kicks, body spins and spectacular catches. Whether solo or in a team – in freestyle, 'flow' is everything. It's a game with physics, where wind and spin are used to create impossible flight paths. It's not just about difficulty, but also about personal style and acrobatic ease.

The World Championships in Munich

When the global elite comes together for the World Championships in Munich, the playing field transforms into a stage for peak athletic performance. In choreographed routines, athletes compete to music, with an expert jury evaluating performances according to strict criteria. Technical difficulty of maneuvers, precision of execution, and artistic presentation and musicality all factor into the scoring. But these World Championships are far more than a pure competition: they are the vibrant center of a passionate community, where innovation is celebrated and the "Spirit of the Game" — respect and the joy of playing together — always comes first.
